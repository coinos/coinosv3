Bitcoin you actually hold: your wallet is twelve words, nobody else ever has them, and they work in any wallet, anywhere.

* Spending over Ark: instant, low-fee off-chain payments with a unilateral exit — your keys, your coins
* Savings on-chain: a full BIP84 bitcoin wallet
* Lightning sends and receives via the coinos Ark server, plus a name@coinos.io payment address (BIP-353, LNURL, BOLT 12)
* Nostr built in: encrypted DMs, community chat, posts, zaps
* Handles lightning:, bitcoin:, nostr: and lnurl: links and NFC taps

This build carries the whole app inside the APK. The coinos servers are only reached for their APIs (the Ark server, Electrum, the name registrar).
